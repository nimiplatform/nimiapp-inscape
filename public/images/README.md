# Inner landscape

`inner-landscape.webp` is the project-owned raster used by the Today and welcome screens. It was created with the built-in ImageGen tool, then encoded as WebP for the local application (about 166 KB). The app loads it locally.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide editorial illustration for Inscape, a warm private desktop self-exploration journal, used on a 950 by 275 pixel dashboard hero. Create a refined atmospheric painterly illustration, panoramic 3:1 aspect ratio. Quiet surreal botanical landscape of a small sunlit terracotta arch doorway standing in a rolling sage and moss green meadow, a winding cream path leads toward it, a beautiful softly sculptural deep-green tree on the right, tiny ivory wildflowers, warm golden orb of the late-afternoon sun in a pale butter sky. Subtle grain of colored-pencil and gouache on paper, sophisticated editorial illustration with simplified shapes and very soft light, muted earthy colors, cream #f7f5ee, olive #526248, sage #b5bba0, warm pale yellow #e6dcb2 and pale dusty peach. Composition important: the entire LEFT 50 percent of the picture should be nearly empty warm cream lightly textured sky/ground for live UI headline text; all detailed tree/door/path objects in the RIGHT 50 percent. Ground blends seamlessly into warm cream at left. No text, no people, no labels, no lettering, no UI or borders, no purple, no blue, no high contrast, no sharp vector lines. Evokes curiosity, a world inside you, and a peaceful place to pause. A beautiful expressive illustration not a stock wellness image. Save the generated asset and return its local path.
